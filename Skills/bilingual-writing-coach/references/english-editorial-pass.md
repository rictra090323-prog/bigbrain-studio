# English editorial pass

Use this after writing or editing formal English. Review each paragraph for work it performs in the argument. Replace signposting that merely restates the next sentence. Collapse abstract claims of importance into the evidence and implication the source supports. Vary repeated sentence structures when they flatten the distinction between ideas. Keep useful terminology, qualifications, and authorial cadence. Prefer changes you can explain in editorial terms.

For a checklist of recurring English patterns, see [Humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer). Treat it as a source of revision prompts. User-approved examples and the genre determine which edits serve a particular text.
